caption v3.6

- \LTXtable* innerhalb ltcaption anbieten!?

- \ContinuedFloat neu implementieren.
  \ContinuedFloat erniedrigt nun keinen Zähler mehr, sondern setzt lediglich ein lokales Flag.
  \caption erhöht den Zähler nicht, wenn dieses Flag gesetzt ist (und löscht das Flag dabei).
  \subcaptionbox bzw. \subcaption erhöhen nur dann den Zähler lokal(!), wenn das Flag nicht gesetzt ist, und es noch keine \caption gab.
  Hierbei (also nur in dem "nur dann"-Fall!) wird ein anderes Flag gesetzt, das anzeigt, daß diese Abbildung eine \caption bekommen sollte.
  Ist am Ende der Abbildung das "sollte eine caption haben"-Flag nicht gesetzt, wird der Abbildungszähler erhöht, um das Fehlen der \caption zu kompensieren.
  Vorteil der neuen Lösung: \ContinuedFloat funktioniert auch dann, wenn es in Abbildungen ohne \caption vorkommt.
  Andere Idee:
  Drei Flags: 1. \ContinuedFloat, 2. \caption, 3. \subcaption: Default: aus, an, an
  \ContinuedFloat setzt Flag #1
  \caption erhöht nur dann den Zähler, wenn !#1 und #2 (bei "#1 und #2" wird #1 zurückgesetzt)
  \caption invertiert immer #3
  ... TODO!
  ACHTUNG:
  Ist das subfig-Paket geladen, muß entweder subfig dementsprechend gepatcht werden (was vermutlich nicht trivial geht),
  oder aber \ContinuedFloat muß sich wie früher verhalten, wenn das subfig-Paket geladen ist.

- |listfigurename|  & sets the `List of Figure' name & \ref{names} \\
|figurename|\preambleoption & sets the figure name & \ref{names} \\
|figurewithin|\preambleoption & sets the figure ``within'' counter & \ref{numbering} \\
\DescribeMacro{figurename=}
\DescribeMacro{tablename=}\NEWfeature{v3.0l}
You can change the name of the figures and tables with the options
\begin{quote}\begin{tabular}{@{}r@{}l@{\quad}l@{}}
  |figurename=| & \meta{name} & and\\
  |tablename=|  & \meta{name} & .\\
\end{tabular}\end{quote}
The document class usually sets them to ``Figure'' and ``Table'', but for
example with
\begin{quote}
  |figurename=Fig.|
\end{quote}
you can change the figure name to ``Fig''.

\smallskip\INFO
If you use the \package{babel} package, please load \thispackage\ \emph{after}
the \package{babel} package.

\medskip

\pagebreak[3]
\DescribeMacro{listfigurename=}
\DescribeMacro{listtablename=}\NEWfeature{v3.1g}
Analogous to the options |figure|\-|name| and |table|\-|name| you can change
the title of the lists here. So for example
\begin{quote}
  |listfigurename=List of images|
\end{quote}
changes the title ``List of Figures'' to ``List of images''.

\bigskip

\pagebreak[3]
\DescribeMacro{figurewithin=}
\DescribeMacro{tablewithin=}\NEWfeature{v3.1f}
If a document class providing |\chapter| is used (e.g.~\class{report} or
\class{book}), usually the |figure| and |table| caption counters
are dependent on the |chapter| counter. Otherwise usually the |figure| and
|table| counters are continuous throughout the document.

This can be changed with the options
\begin{quote}\begin{tabular}{rl}
  |figurewithin=| & \texttt{chapter} \emph{or} \texttt{section} \emph{or} \texttt{none}\quad and\\
  |tablewithin=|  & \texttt{chapter} \emph{or} \texttt{section} \emph{or} \texttt{none}\quad .\\
\end{tabular}\end{quote}
If ``|none|'' is given as value, the numbering of figures resp. tables will
be continuous throughout the document.
Otherwise the numbering will be dependent on the given counter,
e.g.~|figure|\-|within=|\x|section| gives figures a section-dependent
numbering scheme.

\bigskip

\begin{Expert*}
The name and numbering is internally done by the commands
|\|\meta{type}|name| \& |\the|\meta{type}, e.g.~|\figure|\-|name| \&
|\the|\-|figure| for figures.
While usually using the above options are sufficient, sometimes a direct
re-definition of these two macros is necessary. For example:
\begin{quote}
  |\captionsetup{figurewithin=section}|\\
  |\renewcommand\thefigure{\arabic{section}\alph{figure}}|
\end{quote}
gives you a section-dependent numbering scheme \meta{section}\meta{figure}
with a numerical section counter and a alphanumerical figure counter,
e.g.~``|Figure 2b|''.
\end{Expert*}

- Doc:
\captionlistentry is not performing the task of \endfirstfoot, but you
would not need \captionlistentry if \endfirstfoot would exist. Maybe my
text in the documentation is a little bit misleading here. So here is a
new try:

What you usually want, if you place the captions below the table: You
want only one entry in the List of Tables, and its page number should be
the first page of the longtable. If \endfirstfoot would exist, you could
simply use \caption{...} in the first foot, and \caption[]{...} within
the foots of the other pages. (\caption[]{...} will not produce an entry
to the List of Tables.) But since \endfirstfoot does not exist, you need
to get around this problem in a different way. If you say
\caption[]{...} in every foot you will not get an entry to the list of
tables at all. But combined with \captionlistentry (with makes a list
